# GPT concept-visual generation, rights and limitation note

## Scope and disclosure

The four image files in this directory are **AI-generated conceptual scenario visualizations** for the proposal *Jingzhang Intelligence Spine: A Visible Public AI Innovation Belt*. They communicate atmosphere, material character, public-realm ambition and the relationship between people, blue-green space, accessible mobility and governed AI services. They are not photographs, base-map evidence, surveys, measurements, legal plans, official renderings, approvals, engineering documents, statements of public support, or depictions of completed construction.

The model and prompts were directed to use generic, non-identifiable adult figures and to avoid trademarks, real people, logos, maps, signage and claims of existing or approved conditions. The scenes should be read together with the package’s structured GeoJSON, metrics, sources, assumptions, figures and drawings; those evidence artifacts, not the synthetic images, support any stated spatial metrics or proposal claims.

## Rights, accessibility and use limitations

The participant created the prompts and selected the images for this submission. No third-party photograph, human likeness, voice, music, trademark, map tile or private-interior source was supplied to the generation process. The images include useful alt text in both the Markdown report and offline visual pages. They contain no essential text. Distribution is limited to the repository’s declared community-display package license and the applicable terms of the image-generation service. Any future public reuse must undergo an independent rights, branding, accessibility and factual-claims review.

> The images do not establish any project boundary, land ownership, height, floor area, public approval, service operation, engineering feasibility or construction commitment. Official and professional materials remain required for all such determinations.
